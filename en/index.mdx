[OmniMux · omnimux.ai](https://omnimux.ai/)

OmniMux helps you create AI characters, build multi-view reference sheets, and generate character videos from reference clips.

## Put your character in any trend

Create a character, pick a reference video, and generate your own character video. Reuse your character across different video ideas.

- **Create a character** from a description or feature choices. Reference photos can be used as character material for a video.
- **Build multi-view references** from your own successfully generated character image to guide later creations.
- **Generate a character video** with character assets and a reference clip you have the rights to use. View results in your history and download them.

Start in the [character workspace](https://omnimux.ai/influencer). Browse looks and try traits before signing in to upload or generate. Upload photos and reference videos in the workspace, rather than on the homepage.

## Start with a reference video

1. Sign in to the character workspace and upload character reference images, which can include multi-view references, and a short reference video.
2. Describe the character features, action order, framing, and scene details you want to preserve, then submit the generation.
3. When it finishes, open Preview in your history, play the result, and check the picture before downloading.

### Completed in-app example: a 4-second character video

One completed in-app record used two character reference images and a 4-second reference video. Its direction asked to preserve the character's outfit and the order of alternating steps and arm movements, use fixed full-body framing, and make only light scene changes.

The task showed a successful status, and its preview played: the output was 480 × 854 and about 4 seconds long, showing a character in an orange jacket stepping and moving her arms.
